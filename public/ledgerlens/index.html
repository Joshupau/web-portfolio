LedgerLens

Import a bank or e-wallet statement and LedgerLens proves, with arithmetic, whether it adds up. When it doesn't, it shows you the line and proposes a fix. Nothing changes until you approve it.

By Joshua De Guzman, built to plug into Fico, a finance tracker. Read how it works or see more of my work. Every statement and message here is invented and processed in your browser.

      How it works

      1. Read the statement. The CSV is parsed into whole centavos, never floating-point pesos, so ₱0.10 + ₱0.20 is exactly ₱0.30. Rows it can't read are listed, not dropped.
      2. Check the arithmetic. With a running balance, every row must move the balance by exactly its amount, so each break is pinned to a line. With only opening and closing balances, one gap is found and its likely causes are ranked.
      3. You decide. Each finding comes with a proposed fix. Nothing is applied until a person approves it, and every approved fix is recorded with who approved it and when.

      What it can't do yet

      • Read PDF statements. Only CSV for now.
      • Tell a wrong digit from a missing row when both fit the numbers. It offers both and asks.
      • See errors that cancel out when the statement has no balance column.
      • Claim real-world accuracy. The benchmarks use invented statements and 120 hand-written messages.

      LedgerLens is built in TypeScript with no runtime dependencies. The model is an averaged perceptron trained from scratch on synthetic Taglish messages in about ten seconds.
